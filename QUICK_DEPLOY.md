## 🚀 Fastest Way to Publish Your Website

### Step 1: Open Netlify Drop
Go to: **https://app.netlify.com/drop**

### Step 2: Drag & Drop
Find the **`dist`** folder in your project and drag it into the browser

### Step 3: Done! 🎉
Your site is LIVE with a URL like: `https://your-site-name.netlify.app`
